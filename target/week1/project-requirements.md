# Campus Placement and Internship Portal
## Week 1 - Project Requirements

### 1. Project Title

Campus Placement and Internship Portal

---

## 2. Introduction

The Campus Placement and Internship Portal is a web-based application
designed to simplify and manage the placement and internship process
within a college.

The system provides a common platform where students can view available
job and internship opportunities and apply for suitable opportunities.
Recruiters can manage job openings and applications, while administrators
can manage the overall system.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

The traditional placement process often involves manual communication,
spreadsheets, notices and multiple sources of information.

This can make it difficult for students to track opportunities and
application status and can also make it difficult for recruiters and
administrators to manage placement-related information.

The proposed system provides a centralized digital platform to manage
students, recruiters, job opportunities and applications.

---

## 4. Project Objectives

The main objectives of the project are:

- To develop a centralized placement and internship management system.
- To provide secure login and registration facilities.
- To allow students to create and manage their profiles.
- To allow students to view available jobs and internships.
- To provide an online application facility.
- To allow students to track their application status.
- To allow recruiters to manage job and internship opportunities.
- To help recruiters manage student applications.
- To provide administrators with system management facilities.
- To reduce manual work in the placement process.

---

## 5. Target Users

The system will mainly have three types of users:

### Student

Students can:

- Register and login.
- Create and manage their profile.
- View available jobs and internships.
- Apply for suitable opportunities.
- Track application status.

### Recruiter

Recruiters can:

- Register and login.
- Manage company information.
- Post job and internship opportunities.
- View applications.
- Manage candidates.

### Administrator

Administrators can:

- Manage students.
- Manage recruiters and companies.
- Manage job and internship postings.
- Monitor applications.
- Manage the overall portal.

---

## 6. Functional Requirements

### 6.1 User Registration

The system shall allow users to create an account using the required
registration information.

### 6.2 User Login

The system shall provide secure login functionality for registered users.

### 6.3 Role-Based Access

The system shall provide different functionality based on the user's role.

Roles include:

- Student
- Recruiter
- Administrator

### 6.4 Student Profile

Students shall be able to create and update their personal and academic
information.

### 6.5 Job and Internship Management

The system shall provide facilities to display available job and
internship opportunities.

### 6.6 Job Application

Students shall be able to apply for suitable job and internship
opportunities.

### 6.7 Application Tracking

Students shall be able to view the status of their submitted applications.

### 6.8 Recruiter Management

Recruiters shall be able to create and manage job and internship postings.

### 6.9 Application Management

Recruiters shall be able to view and manage applications received from
students.

### 6.10 Administration

Administrators shall be able to manage users and placement-related
information.

---

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements

### Performance

The system should respond to user requests within a reasonable amount
of time.

### Security

User authentication and passwords should be handled securely.

### Reliability

The system should provide consistent results and prevent invalid
database operations.

### Usability

The interface should be simple and easy to understand.

### Maintainability

The application should have a modular structure so that new features
can be added easily.

### Compatibility

The system should work with commonly used web browsers.

## 9. Project Scope

The project will cover:

- User registration and authentication.
- Student profile management.
- Recruiter/company management.
- Job and internship management.
- Online applications.
- Application status tracking.
- Administrative management.
- Database-driven information storage.

The system is intended to provide a centralized platform for managing
the college placement and internship workflow.

---

## 10. Expected Outcome

At the end of the project, a functional web-based Campus Placement and
Internship Portal will be developed.

The system will allow students, recruiters and administrators to perform
their respective activities through a centralized platform.
